# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A 25-34-year-old salaried, phone-first heavy BNPL user, like Lena, who has several pay-later apps and no provider loyalty.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Have money leave her account only after she has decided to keep the item, without having to track it all herself.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** She opens the card screen and the pay later screen, sees two different totals, and has to work out which one is real (T-103, N-07). Two reminders then arrive in the same week for different amounts (T-104), and a third thing to check before payday is exactly what she feared (N-02).
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** payment with another debit card
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** 1	Opens the card screen and the pay later screen separately and asks which total is real
2	Compares two reminders and two due dates that don’t match, to work out what leaves her account when
3	Decides at checkout using whichever provider feels predictable, since Riverty’s instalment option appears after purchase and only for some categories
4	If a push notification arrives late, assumes the payment failed and pays again with another card
5	After an unexplained decline, goes back to her debit card
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** Compares two reminders and two due dates that don’t match, to work out what leaves her account when
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** T-104,ticket,Statement date and pay later due dates differ. Two reminders arrive in the same week for different amounts.
